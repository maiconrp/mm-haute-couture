Legal & Protection

Privacy Policy

1. Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. It is Márcia Morais Haute Couture's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our website and private fitting studio.

2. Information Collection

To arrange a fitting, we collect your name, email, phone/WhatsApp, selected service, appointment time, and optional event date. For a mobile fitting, we also collect the address or area you provide. With your consent, booking details are sent to the atelier's authorized Google Calendar account to check availability and record confirmed appointments.

3. Data Retention & Security

Confirmed appointment details remain in the atelier's Google Calendar until removed by the atelier. The website stores a limited booking reference outside its public files to prevent duplicate submissions. The calendar and booking data are protected using server-side credentials and access controls.

4. Analytics, Advertising & Third-Party Services

If configured, and only with your optional consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure website visits and interactions, and Meta Pixel to measure advertising performance and contact or confirmed booking actions. These services may receive pseudonymous browser, device, page and campaign information; we do not send booking form details such as your name, email, phone number, address or message to them. Optional tags remain disabled until you consent. Your preference is stored in browser local storage for up to 180 days and can be changed using the Privacy settings control on the site. We also use Google Calendar to process appointment availability and confirmed bookings as described above, and may disclose information where required by law.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about how we handle user data and personal information, feel free to contact us via WhatsApp at +61 401 185 923.
