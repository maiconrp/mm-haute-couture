September 2026 | Brisbane

Brazil Week Fashion Day 2026

At Brazil Week Fashion Day 2026, Márcia Morais took part as a guest stylist, bringing her couture perspective to a celebration of Brazilian creativity and culture in Queensland.

Her participation highlighted the atelier's artisanal approach, precision in finishing, and attention to detail across each look presented on the runway.

More than just showcasing Brazilian fashion, the experience represented an opportunity to value the national culture and strengthen ties between Brazil and Australia, reaffirming the essence of Márcia Morais Haute Couture: elegance, exclusivity, and excellence in every detail.
